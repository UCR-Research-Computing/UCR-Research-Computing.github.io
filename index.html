Know before you start

Four things every researcher should know

Short, plain answers. Each links to the policy or rate sheet that governs it.

Security and data

Your data has a protection level

UC classifies information P1 to P4. The level decides where it may be stored and processed.

Some services carry a recharge

Rates come from published rate sheets. The sheet in force when you are billed is the one that applies.

Shared systems have fair-use limits

Quotas, queues and priorities protect everyone, and can change as demand changes.

Backups are not archives

Know which storage is backed up, which is not, and how long you are required to keep your data.
