## What it is

The National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot is a federal program, led by the National Science Foundation with partner agencies and companies. It connects US researchers to AI computing, datasets, models and software. Resources are awarded through calls for proposals, and the available systems, tracks and allocation sizes change between calls.

## Cost

UCR does not recharge for NAIRR Pilot allocations. They are awarded competitively by the program, and awards depend on its review and available capacity.

## How to get started

Read the current opportunities on the [NAIRR Pilot portal](https://nairrpilot.org/). [KB008]({{ '/kb/kb008-using-nairr-pilot/' | relative_url }}) describes how UCR researchers have used it. Research Computing can help you benchmark on campus systems and prepare the technical part of a request. [Contact us]({{ '/help/' | relative_url }}).
