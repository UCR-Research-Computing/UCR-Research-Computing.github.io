## What it is

ACCESS (Advanced Cyberinfrastructure Coordination Ecosystem: Services and Support) is the National Science Foundation's program for national research computing. It awards allocations on supercomputers, GPU systems, storage and research clouds run by resource providers around the country. It continues the work of the earlier XSEDE program.

## Project types

ACCESS offers four project sizes. **Explore** suits evaluating resources, student projects, classes and code development. **Discover** suits modest grant-funded needs. **Accelerate** suits mid-scale needs. **Maximize** is for the largest requests, reviewed on a fixed schedule. Starting small and growing is common. ACCESS publishes the current limits and review cycles on its [allocations site](https://allocations.access-ci.org/).

## Cost

UCR does not recharge for ACCESS allocations, and ACCESS awards them rather than selling them. Each resource provider's own policies apply to how an allocation may be used.
