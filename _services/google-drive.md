## What it is

Google Workspace (Google Drive) and Microsoft OneDrive are campus storage services managed by ITS. They are built for documents and collaboration, not large datasets.

## What is included

ITS currently lists Google Drive at {% include fact.html id="google_drive_default" %}, and OneDrive at {% include fact.html id="onedrive_default" bare=true %}. The [ITS storage page](https://its.ucr.edu/storage) is the authority and covers file size limits, retention after deletion, sharing outside UCR, and supported clients.

## Keep in mind

For large or binary research data, use [project storage]({{ '/storage/' | relative_url }}). For data with a protection level above P2, check what your data security plan allows before storing it anywhere.
