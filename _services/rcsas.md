## What it is

The Research Computing System Administration Service (RCSAS) designs, builds and administers lab-owned research clusters and servers. It brings them to a consistent, supportable configuration that is designed to meet UC security standards at P2 or higher.

## What is in and out of scope

In scope, under an MOU: architecture and deployment of lab research systems, standard identity and access management, software lifecycle management, and configuration to UC P2 controls.

Out of scope unless an MOU says otherwise: backup and recovery, supplying new or replacement hardware, and P4-level controls.

The [RCSAS MOU](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eD8p5CLpMsq6gdfAoHnx_xoY0i_3Ovha2IBrEhiYaIo/edit) template shows the full division of responsibilities between Research Computing and the lab.

## How to start

[Contact Research Computing]({{ '/help/' | relative_url }}) to describe the system and what you need. If RCSAS is a fit, the engagement is set out in an MOU before work begins.
