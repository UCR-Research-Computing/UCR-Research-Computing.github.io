## How to read this site

- **Figures.** Prices, quotas and hardware figures are copied from the source named beside each one, as of the date shown. The source document is the authority. If a figure here differs from its source, the source is correct.
- **Status labels.** *Available* means a service is offered now under its published terms. *Pilot* means terms and capacity may change, and allocations are reviewed individually. *By review* means access depends on an assessment of your project. *By application* means a separate program awards access. *External* means another organization runs the service.
- **"No recharge under current terms"** describes the present arrangement only. It depends on continued funding and is subject to eligibility and limits.
- **Suggestions** from the resource finder, the catalog, or a consultation are guidance, not an offer of service.
- **Other units' services.** Where a service is run by the HPCC, ITS or a national program, its own terms and policies apply.
