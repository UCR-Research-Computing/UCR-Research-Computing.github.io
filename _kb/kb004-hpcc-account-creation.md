HPCC accounts are created by the High-Performance Computing Center. The HPCC [Access page](https://hpcc.ucr.edu/about/overview/access/) is the authority on the process. In short:

1. **Email the HPCC** at support@hpcc.ucr.edu. Include the full name, NetID and email address of the user and of the PI.
2. **Involve the PI.** Users must be members of the PI's group. The request should come from the PI, or the PI should be copied on it.
3. **New labs** that are not yet registered include, in the same email, a funding source (COA) for the annual lab registration ({% include fact.html id="hpcc_lab_fee" bare=true %}), the email of the lab's financial contact, and any additional storage wanted.
4. **Log in.** When you receive your account details, follow the HPCC [login instructions](https://hpcc.ucr.edu/manuals/access/login/).

Then try [connecting and running a first job](../how-to-connect-to-hpc-cluster-run-sample-job/).
