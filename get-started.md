## Five steps

<div class="steps vertical">
  <div class="step"><h4>Tell us what you do</h4><p>Your field, your data, and what you want to run. If you are not sure what you need, that is fine. <a href="{{ '/help/' | relative_url }}">Ask for a consultation</a>.</p></div>
  <div class="step"><h4>Check your data</h4><p>Find its <a href="{{ '/security/' | relative_url }}#data-protection-levels">protection level</a>, and any agreement (data use, contract, IRB) attached to it. This decides where it can go.</p></div>
  <div class="step"><h4>Pick a service</h4><p>Compare <a href="{{ '/compute/' | relative_url }}">compute</a> and <a href="{{ '/storage/' | relative_url }}">storage</a> options. Each service page says what it is for, what it is not for, and what it costs.</p></div>
  <div class="step"><h4>Get access</h4><p>Follow the service's access steps. Usually your PI approves, an account is created, and a funding source is set up if the service carries a recharge.</p></div>
  <div class="step"><h4>Run and learn</h4><p>Use the guides below and the <a href="{{ '/kb/' | relative_url }}">knowledge base</a>, and come to office hours when you get stuck.</p></div>
</div>

## Workshops and courses

Research Computing and the HPCC run workshops from time to time; announcements go out on [Slack](https://ucr-research-compute.slack.com/). Good self-paced options:

- [The Carpentries](https://carpentries.org/): Software, Data and Library Carpentry lessons on the shell, Git, Python and R.
- [HPC Carpentry](https://www.hpc-carpentry.org/): introductory HPC lessons.
- [Cornell Virtual Workshop](https://cvw.cac.cornell.edu/): HPC and cloud topics, used across the national ACCESS community.
- [ACCESS training and events](https://support.access-ci.org/events): national webinars and workshops.
