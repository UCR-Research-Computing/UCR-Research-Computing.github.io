Research Computing is part of UC Riverside's Information Technology Solutions (ITS). We help researchers find and use computing, storage and data services, on campus, in the cloud, and through national programs.

## What we do

- **Consultation and facilitation.** We help researchers choose services, plan projects and grants, and move work between systems.
- **Services.** We run [Ursa Major]({{ '/services/ursa-major/' | relative_url }}) (UCR's Google Cloud research program), [CephRDS]({{ '/services/cephrds/' | relative_url }}) storage, the [secure research enclave]({{ '/services/secure-enclave/' | relative_url }}) with ITS and the Information Security Office, and [lab system administration]({{ '/services/rcsas/' | relative_url }}).
- **Guidance.** This site and its [knowledge base]({{ '/kb/' | relative_url }}).

## Who we work with

- The **High-Performance Computing Center (HPCC)** runs the campus cluster and its storage. The [HPCC website](https://hpcc.ucr.edu/) is the authority on its systems, rates and policies.
- **ITS** provides campus accounts, collaboration storage, campus AI tools and cloud agreements. See [its.ucr.edu](https://its.ucr.edu/).
- The **Information Security Office** sets campus security requirements and reviews data security plans.
- National programs, including **NSF ACCESS**, the **NAIRR Pilot**, the **National Research Platform** and **OSG**.
