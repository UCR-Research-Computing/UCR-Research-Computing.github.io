Using NAIRR Pilot for AI Research at UC Riverside

Zero-Cost Federal H100 GPU Clusters, Wafer-Scale AI Accelerators & Foundation Model APIs

1. Executive Summary: What is the NAIRR Pilot?

The National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot is a $100M+ federal initiative led by the National Science Foundation (NSF) and Department of Energy (DOE) providing zero-cost access to high-performance AI computing, specialized hardware, and foundation model APIs for U.S. researchers.

Through UCR Research Computing, faculty can leverage NAIRR to scale AI workloads beyond local campus resources (HPCC and GCP Ursa Major) into national exascale supercomputers and specialized AI accelerators at no charge to their grants.

2. NAIRR Resource Portfolio (What You Can Request)

Resource Category Available Hardware & Platforms Ideal Research Use Case
Exotic AI Accelerators Cerebras CS-3, SambaNova SN40L, Groq LPU Wafer-scale AI training, ultra-low latency inference, & non-GPU chip architectures.
Massive H100/A100 GPU Clusters NCSA DeltaAI (1,200+ H100s), SDSC Expanse, TACC Vista Multi-node distributed LLM pre-training, fine-tuning, & large vision models.
Exascale Supercomputers ORNL Frontier (DOE Exascale), ALCF Aurora Climate AI, molecular dynamics, & massive multi-physics simulation campaigns.
Foundation Model APIs OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Vertex AI, Hugging Face Prompt engineering, evaluation benchmarks, & fine-tuning proprietary models.

3. Allocation Tracks & Application Paths

⚡ Track 1: NAIRR Startup (Fast-Track On-Ramp)

Best For: Benchmarking, code testing, and gathering preliminary data for grant proposals.

Review Time: Fast-tracked (1–2 weeks) with a 2-page proposal.

Allocation: Up to 5,000 GPU-hours or $5,000 in model API credits.

🏆 Track 2: NAIRR Research (Full Campaign)

Best For: Full research campaigns, large-scale LLM training, and paper publications.

Review Time: Monthly peer-review cycle.

Allocation: Up to 100,000+ GPU-hours or Cerebras wafer-scale cluster access.

4. UCR Research Computing Onboarding Support

UCR Research Computing acts as your institutional bridge to NAIRR Pilot allocations:

  • Proposal Review & Benchmarking: We assist UCR faculty in running scaling benchmarks on HPCC or GCP Ursa Major to include in your NAIRR application.
  • Access & Credential Mapping: Once granted, we help configure Globus endpoint transfers, SSH key authentication, and Slurm job scripts for DeltaAI, Expanse, or Cerebras.
  • Hybrid Workflow Integration: Run pre-processing on HPCC/Ursa Major, launch massive GPU training on NAIRR DeltaAI, and store results in CephRDS S3.
